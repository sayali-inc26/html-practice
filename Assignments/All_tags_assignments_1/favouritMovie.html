Movie Story

Story Summary

Kaho Naa... Pyaar Hai (2000) is a romantic thriller about an aspiring singer named Rohit and a wealthy girl, Sonia, who fall in love but are torn apart when Rohit is murdered after witnessing a crime. A devastated Sonia is sent to New Zealand to heal, where she meets Rohit's wealthy lookalike, Raj, who falls for her and returns to India to help her uncover the truth. Together, they stage a trap that exposes the corrupt criminals, culminating in the shocking twist that Sonia's own father was the ultimate mastermind behind Rohit's death.
